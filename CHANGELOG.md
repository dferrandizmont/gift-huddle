# Changelog

All notable changes to Gift Huddle are listed here. Store versions may skip numbers.

## Unreleased

## 1.0.8 · 2026-11-27

- The "Who gives" tab opens faster with long lists.
- Saving and opening large lists is smoother, and unlocking with the PIN is faster.
- Changes you make on this phone are kept when you stop syncing or when an invitation that did not work is cleared.
- Starting a new list on this phone keeps the latest phone name, and never brings back gifts that were hidden from you to keep a surprise.

## 1.0.7 · 2026-11-26

- On mobile and web, Budget uses the actual price when a gift has no planned price. It includes visible, priced plans for claimed wishes when their giver is unassigned or counts as household spending. Wish plans for people whose gifts are hidden on the device stay out of the budget figures.
- A person's page now shows two strips tracking what has been bought, using the actual price when available and the planned price otherwise. The person's strip counts all their visible gifts, whoever gives them. "Household spending · total" follows the Budget rules: it includes gifts from active givers marked "Counts as household spending" (formerly "Household pays") and visible, priced plans for claimed wishes with no giver assigned. The bars never turn red, and both show bought progress in place of the old "spent of planned" figure.
- The delete confirmation counts visible wishes. Deleting a person clears their wishes, secret gifts and plans, and web inbox changes while keeping wishes they suggested for others. Connected devices protect their person from archiving or deletion, even when a web deletion is already queued or pairing is still incomplete. The web repairs wishes left behind by older phones and says whether a connected device or someone else's secret gifts prevented a deletion. Recovery code creation and pending pairing can finish on startup even if the list contains deleted people.
- A free wish on someone else's list is now as short as a gift: "I'll give this" is a compact button in the person's colour on the right of the same line (in the status column on tablets and the web), with the wish label and detail on the second line; "Nobody has taken it" is gone when there is a button. With large text the button drops below the text, never cut off. A screen reader now announces it as "I'll give this: <wish>".
- A person's page folds its budget card, filters and summary line while you scroll down, leaving the avatar and the name, and brings them back when you scroll up (with Reduce motion it only fades). The summary under the name now breaks into two clean lines, with the "·" always at the end of a line, also with large text.
- The "Add gift" button no longer sits over what you are typing or searching: it hides while the keyboard is open and while searching, and comes back afterwards.
- On a person's page the "+" button hides while you scroll down the list and comes back when you scroll up or reach the end (it stays put with a screen reader or when the list does not scroll).
- Taking a wish from a phone that is not yet connected to the family with the new version now says "To take wishes, this phone has to be connected to the family with the new version." (it used to talk about secret gifts), no longer covers the button, and offers "How to connect it", which opens Family phones.
- Gift titles and other web text now use Flutter's full set of fallback fonts, served with the app. This covers emoji, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hebrew, Thai, Devanagari, mathematical symbols, and more.
- Phone and web pairing avoid long polling gaps caused by the inviting phone using up its request budget too early.
- Deleting a giver on the web leaves their wish plans unassigned on both web and mobile, while keeping the plan details.
- Turning a wish plan into a surprise after its giver was deleted keeps the surprise unassigned and preserves the plan details.
- The classic web People list counts claimed wishes as gifts.
- After stopping sync, People and the person's header count the wishes still visible on that phone.
- Switching between "Not on the list" and "Nobody" during setup starts a fresh name entry for the selected option.
- Android: Gift Huddle moves to a new Google Play app, `es.gifthuddle.android`. It installs as a separate app next to the old one (`es.gifthuddle.app`), so the list has to be moved with the recovery code; the iPhone app does not change.
- Website (gifthuddle.app): the top button now just says "Download" (no note beside it).
- Website (gifthuddle.app) and README: Android is available on Google Play as the new app es.gifthuddle.android; the closed-test tester steps are gone, and the App Store badge stays.
- The secret gifts block no longer appears on other people's birthdays (only on the birthday of the person whose gifts are secret, and on Christmas, Kings and the other shared occasions), and its icon and text are now centered inside the card instead of sitting on its top edge.
- Own wishes: each person with a phone (or the web) can note down what they would like to get. Their wishes appear in their list next to the gifts, marked "Wish", and the person sees and edits only the wish itself, never who takes it, its status, prices, store or notes. The rest of the family sees whether a wish is free, or who took it and its details like any other secret gift (only the person who took it can change or release it), or, if two people took it, a warning to release one; "I'll give it" takes a wish, and the person who took it edits giver, status, prices, store and notes like a normal gift, can release it or turn it into a separate surprise. If the person removes a wish someone had taken, that gift stays with "Nora removed it from her list". When adding a gift for someone with a phone, the new "Visible to Nora" switch (off by default) makes it a wish she can see instead of a surprise; for people without a phone nothing changes. The fixed block on the person's own page is now called "Surprises", always there and without numbers or dates. Search, People and the occasion pages also list wishes; removing a wish, releasing a taken wish and deleting a claim ask first. Up to 200 wishes per person. Only the person who took a wish can change, release, delete or turn it into a surprise. Some wish actions exist only on the phone, not on the web: suggesting a wish with "Visible to Nora", turning a taken wish into a surprise and deleting a claim; the web can add, edit and remove its own person's wishes and take, edit and release other people's wishes. On the web a wish someone took stays in its occasion and in Search, showing who took it. Taking wishes works only when every phone of the family has this latest version; with an older phone the app says so and saves nothing until it is updated.
- On other people's phones, People, the person's page, Occasions and the occasion pages count free wishes apart from gifts ("Nora · 2 gifts · 4 wishes"), and an occasion that only has wishes shows how many instead of "No gifts yet". A wish someone has taken shows only the "Wish" label, never also "Surprise". "Turn into a surprise" now keeps the prices, store and notes you typed but had not saved, a purchase link that is not a link is flagged in its own field, and a very long wish title is shortened with "…" in the notices and confirmation questions. The add button on your own page is read as "Add wish", the "Surprises" label in Who gives what no longer splits in the middle of a word, and the help text under the phone name on the welcome screen wraps fully with large text.
- Connecting another phone (and the web): when "Who are you?" or "Who is the new phone for?" asks for a name ("Not on the list" or "Nobody"), the name field and "Continue" now stay above the keyboard together, also when the note about secret gifts appears and with large text, instead of "Continue" hiding behind it (the sheet scrolls by itself to keep it in view).
- Wishes, second final review: choosing your own person in "For whom" in any "Add gift" form opens the wish form carrying the title, occasion, size, link and description (never the private note or prices) instead of saving a gift for yourself, and from an occasion the wish form opens straight away only if you are the only person on the list; on a person whose secret block this phone cannot open, "Suggest a wish" opens the form with "Visible for" fixed on and the surprise switch off with its reason; in "Who gives what" nobody but the person who took a wish can change who gives it; a wish plan whose giver was deleted shows "Unassigned"; the status picker and the plan form hide their content as soon as access is revoked or being recalculated; deleting a giver that other phones cannot handle yet says they need the latest version; on the desktop web, an occasion's board includes the wishes taken on the web.
- Web: the "shared computer" notice that appears with secret gifts on screen is shown only until you tap "Understood": it no longer comes back after the 5-minute lock or a reload in that browser, and it appears again only after "Forget this computer" and pairing it anew.
- A person with no gifts but with wishes now shows only "1 wish" / "4 wishes" in People and in the person's header, instead of "No gifts · 1 wish"; with gifts and wishes it stays "2 gifts · 1 wish".
- Connecting another phone: typing in "Not on the list" a name that already exists in the family (ignoring capitals, accents and extra spaces) asks "Someone called Leo is already on the list. Is that you?" (when the inviting phone chooses, "Is that the same person?") with "That's me" and "Someone else", instead of silently adding a second person with the same name. "That's me" picks the existing person and goes through the usual approval; "Someone else" adds the new one as before.
- Accessibility: a taken wish (and any gift row) on a person's page is now announced as a button with its title, label, status and giver, as it was already tappable.
- Web: the people in the board's sidebar now show the wishes still free, in the same format as People ("2 gifts · 1 wish", or just "1 wish"), following the same privacy rules: wishes this phone could not see do not appear.
- iPhone: in "Who are you?" and "Who is the new phone for?" on the screens that are not a sheet (sharing the link, connecting the web), the on-screen keyboard no longer hides "Continue" (the list scrolls by itself when the keyboard opens).
- A wish from another person's list now opens in a sheet that is as tall as what it holds instead of an almost empty page: it says the occasion, size, description and link, and whether anyone has taken it ("Nobody has taken it", "Aritz took it"), with "I'll give this" as the main button. The person who owns the wish never sees the status or who took it.
- The "New gift" and "Edit gift" form keeps the essentials in view (gift, for whom, who gives, occasion, status, planned price, "It's a request" and "It's a surprise") and tucks the rest (actual price, store, link, size, note) into a "More details" section that opens with a spring, opens by itself when you edit a gift that already has any of those, and opens when the actual price is not a valid amount. Saving and validation work as before.
- iPhone and iPad: the edge swipe in the gift and wish forms now asks "Discard changes?" when there are unsaved changes (and closes the form when there are none), like the back arrow, instead of doing nothing.
- The wish form says "You have unsaved changes to this wish" (it said "gift").

## 1.0.6 · 2026-11-22

- A phone never shows gifts for its own person while other devices are connected; the phone that invites now chooses who the new phone is for, and gifts for that person become secret before the new phone joins; only another person's phone can turn this off. The new phone joins already assigned and approved, without choosing anything or a second approval, and an updated phone refuses to join from a phone that still lets it in without a person (both phones need the latest version).
- Before inviting another phone or the web, a phone without a person asks "Who are you?": pick someone from the list, "I am not on the list" (adds you with the name from the welcome screen) or "Nobody" (someone who only gives gifts and never appears as a recipient). On a phone that has never been connected, choosing needs no approval.
- Every person with a connected phone now has their gifts secret automatically, and a phone that asks for its own protection no longer waits for someone to request it.
- A phone left on its own (after stopping sync) shows and lets you add gifts for its person again; gifts that were secret are not on it and do not come back.
- A phone left on its own because the only other phone stopped syncing or was removed keeps showing the secret gifts for everyone else that it already had, and you can still add and edit them; gifts for its own person never come back.
- On the store version, a new installation no longer joins with the old pairing QR that carried the family key (it joined without a person); the other phone needs the latest version.
- After scanning the QR from the welcome screen, the next screen is titled "Connect this phone" instead of "Join with a link", and going back returns to the scanner.
- "For whom is the new phone?" and "Who are you?" are now one screen: tap a person (or "Not on the list" / "Nobody") and it is marked with a tick, the name field and the note that the person's gifts become secret unfold under the list, and "Continue" starts. The phone that joins shows a note saying its person was chosen by the other phone, and its final sheet says who the phone belongs to and that it will not show that person's gifts.
- A person's own page on their phone shows a block that says their gifts are not visible on this phone and that only another person can change it; it never says how many gifts there are. People shows "Gives only" for someone who gives and never receives, and the secret gifts sheet says why the gifts are secret when the person has a connected phone.
- Once, after updating (or when the first other phone connects), a notice tells you that the gifts for your person are no longer shown on this phone; if the phone is later left on its own, another notice says the gifts that were secret are not on it.
- Web: "Waiting for your phone" marks the gifts changed on the web that the phone has not picked up; notices explain when the computer must be connected again from the phone, when the phone has not given it the keys yet, that it locks after 5 minutes with secret gifts on screen, that changes were discarded, and that the server has not accepted the changes for some people yet (retried by itself). "Forget this computer" warns about changes not picked up, and approving the web from the phone warns that it will see and change everyone else's secret gifts.
- Sharing: when the server cannot be reached and the internet provider may be blocking it, the hint now appears under "Changes will be sent later" instead of replacing it.
- Pairing messages: when the other phone still runs 1.0.5 (also a new installation that answers without a person) the host says it needs to update Gift Huddle to join and cancels the invitation instead of waiting; when giving access fails, the message says the other phone was removed and may need to connect again, and that this phone keeps hiding its own person's gifts. The "Waiting for your phone" mark on the web is now the short "Pending" (the full text stays in the tooltip and for screen readers) and comes first among a gift's labels; information notes (who the phone is for, what the web will see, the secret gifts notice) share one soft style, and the magnifier in People is labelled "Search gifts".
- People: the gift search is no longer a field under the title. A square magnifier button next to "+" (same size and shape) unfolds the field under the title with the keyboard open; "Cancel" clears the search and folds it back. Without gifts there is no magnifier. It respects Reduce motion and large text.
- Screenshots: the store version on Android no longer blocks screenshots and recordings everywhere. It blocks them only while a screen that shows gifts is visible (a person's gifts, the gift form, Who gives what, an occasion's detail and search results); Settings, People without a search, Occasions, the welcome screen and the lock screen can be captured. The recovery words and QR stay blocked on every build. Prereleases are unchanged.

- Fixes after testing 1.0.6: turning the phone does not flash a person's own page in its normal layout; the secret gifts sheet on a person's own phone says only another person of the family can change it; Sharing says "Connecting another phone" while a phone waits for approval, and the "Access sent" sheet closes by itself when the other phone finishes; the pending notice names the person the phone chose; People keeps what was drawn while it reloads after "Sync now"; "I am not on the list" and "Nobody" never propose the phone's model as the name, and this phone's person carries a "This phone" note when choosing who a new phone is for; the invitation QR and its code are blocked in screenshots on the store version.
- Build 44: a phone that already had its own list and joins another family with "Replace and join" no longer stays on a blank screen after the codes match; it opens People with the new family's list and shows who the phone belongs to.
- Web: texts about the device itself now say "this computer" instead of "this phone": the secret gifts block ("This computer does not show them"), the own person's block and its one-time notices, "This computer belongs to this person" in Settings › People and its notice, the notices of the gift form and of status changes, the reasons in the gift form, and the privacy line in About.

- Prereleases only: "Connect the web" has a "Paste the QR code" button that reads the QR text from the clipboard (same checks as the camera), so the web can be connected in automated tests without a camera. The store version has neither the button nor that path.
- Web: the web is now an extension of the phone that connects it. It takes that phone's person ("Connect the web" no longer asks whose computer it is) and, like that phone, shows the secret gifts for everyone else, never its own person's. It can add, edit, change the status of and delete those secret gifts; the changes show at once on the web marked "Waiting for your phone" and reach the rest of the family when that phone next syncs. Moving a gift from one person to another while someone is protected is still done from the phone. While secret gifts are on screen the web locks after 5 minutes without use (15 otherwise). The keys exist only while the web is unlocked, and removing the web discards anything its phone had not picked up yet. Connecting a web also hides the gifts for its person first, like a new phone.
- Sync server: when the inviting phone chose the person, an answer without a person (from a 1.0.5 phone) now reaches that phone so it can say the other phone needs to update; an answer for another person is still refused.
- Sync server: a web can store up to 8 encrypted change boxes for its phone to pick up; they count toward the list's limit and are erased as soon as the web is removed.
- Secret gifts: each protected person now holds up to 200 secret gifts (was 50) in the same encrypted block. While another phone of the family still runs 1.0.5 or earlier, only 64 are shared and the rest stay saved on this phone until that phone is updated.
- Sync: if the server cannot be reached, the app now tries a second address on another network route before giving up (5 seconds per address), and remembers the one that worked for 30 minutes. The status now says "Can't connect right now" instead of "Offline", and after three failed attempts in a row it suggests that the internet provider may be blocking the service and to try mobile data or another Wi-Fi.
- Sync server: room for families of up to 10 people with a phone each and everyone protected (90 encrypted blocks, from the first week).
- Texts: deleting a gift now says "Eliminar" in Spanish and Catalan (button, confirmation and notice), like people and occasions; the six digits compared when connecting a phone or the web are always called a "code", never a "number"; the encrypted copy kept for syncing is now called the "encrypted sync copy" (Spanish "copia cifrada para sincronizar") instead of "the copy on the server".
- Texts: clearer wording for the vibrations setting (it controls every vibration in the app), for "This phone belongs to" (it says exactly what the phone stops seeing), for the invitation wait (the phone waits up to 10 minutes for confirmation), for turning off secret gifts, for the budget note, for "Already have a list?" at the start, for "When do you give gifts?" in English, and for the wait while approving the web connection (Spanish).

## 1.0.5 · 2026-11-21

- Texts reviewed in Spanish, Catalan and English: clearer wording and consistent terms.
- Secret gifts no longer offers "Request protection" when no phone in the family could confirm it, and explains what to do instead.
- On iPhone and iPad the lock settings, the PIN screen and the unlock button now say "Face ID" or "Touch ID" (whichever the device has) instead of "fingerprint or face"; Android keeps the generic wording.
- Closing the gift form with unsaved changes now asks "Discard changes?" (back arrow, Close, Cancel, the back gesture, Esc or dragging the sheet down); without changes it closes straight away, and a form that closes itself because the gift is no longer visible still does not ask.
- The sheet drag-to-close now springs back if the screen refuses to close.
- Joining with an invitation link now asks for this phone's name (proposed from its model, editable) before sending the request, so the other phones no longer list it as "Another phone"; a link opened straight from a message shows that step instead of skipping it.
- Pairing: after sending access, the host phone now says the other phone has to complete the connection (Spanish and Catalan used a verb that could read as ending it).
- Delete everything no longer shows a false "server could not be reached" error on a phone whose list was never shared.
- Sharing shows a retryable error and keeps sync on if the server unexpectedly rejects a request to stop syncing.
- Stopping sync checks for old copies from invitations and pairing codes after an app update. If cleanup is interrupted, you can retry from Sharing while sync is off.
- Pending edits upload automatically after a failed stop request, and a pairing QR appears only after the app records that it can be shared.
- Stopping sync keeps unconfirmed remote cleanup retryable if an intermediary sends an unexpected response. A failed attempt to show a pairing QR still lets you delete a local-only family offline.

## 1.0.4 · 2026-11-20

- Secret gifts: the explanation at the top of the sheet now names what it refers to ("With secret gifts on, …") instead of starting with "When this is on".

- Web: deleting a giver or a grouped giver column clears its assignment from gifts that are still waiting for Undo, including after reopening and syncing.
- Gift form: if the gift stops being visible on this phone (for example another phone moves it to a protected person) the form closes itself and any open delete notice closes with it; while the check is uncertain (loading, error or saving) its fields are not drawn. A person chosen as the giver stays chosen until you save, even if they become a giver on another phone meanwhile, and a rejected save says why (gift deleted, giver or person gone or archived, giver and recipient the same). Settings: the delete notice for a giver or person reopens by itself, without anything you can no longer see, if what it lists changes, and unlinking something another phone already changed says so. The "not saved" notice no longer claims another phone deleted the gift: a gift moved out of view says it is no longer available on this phone. A delete notice that cannot recheck what it lists closes with a neutral message.
- Saving a gift or assigning it from Who gives what now checks the latest recipient and protection before restoring a public giver. A stale unlink confirmation leaves the new link intact, and a successful unlink keeps the person's current name.
- Who gives: the giver pickers (gift form, Who gives what sheet and by-occasion list) have a new "People" section so any person in the family can be chosen as the giver. Choosing one only assigns it when you save the form (or straight away in Who gives what), and cancelling creates nothing. A person whose giver was archived is restored instead of duplicated. On a secret gift, a person who is not yet a giver needs an explicit step first ("Add Nora as a giver"), because adding them silently would give the surprise away. The gift form also closes by itself, without saving and with a short notice, if the gift stops being visible on this phone while it is open.
- Settings, Who gives: a giver linked to a person says so and its name is no longer editable there (change it in People); you can link a manual giver to a person or unlink it, with confirmation. The delete confirmation for a giver now names the gifts left without a giver (and any duplicate records), asks again if they changed while it was open, and says a linked person stays in People. Deleting a person who also gives gifts says their name stays on those gifts, and deleting or archiving the person of another phone now explains why it can't be done from here.
- Assigning a person to a secret gift uses an existing public giver. Settings deletes only the giver row you confirm, counts every archived row, and protects people linked to another active phone. Both Who gives what views exclude gifts assigned to archived givers.
- Givers linked to the same person share a column in Who gives what. Each gift keeps its original payer, and gifts someone gave remain attributed to them when their person record is deleted.
- Privacy policy: new "Rating the app" section (from 1.0.4, nothing is stored for ratings until the app is about to ask, then only the count and the date and time of attempts per installation, outside backups; "Delete everything" keeps that count; we can see and reply to published reviews), and gift links are now described as opening, only when you tap them, in your browser or in the app associated with the site.
- Changing who gives a gift no longer reverts a status changed on another device.
- In mobile store builds, the app may ask for a rating when a delivery you mark on this device takes the list from four to five delivered gifts. The app checks that any PIN screen has fully closed and keeps only the number and the date and time of attempts, with at most one every 120 days and three per installation; it only asks Google Play whether the prompt is available when a request is due.
- Privacy policy (20 November 2026): support replies are now sent through Resend, with open and click tracking turned off (no tracking pixel, links left unchanged); Resend keeps a copy of each reply and its sending log for 30 days. Brevo, which added a tracking pixel and rewrote links, was only ever used for the owner's test emails; the policy explains what remains there and when it is deleted.
- Gift links open only from valid HTTPS addresses with ASCII hostnames. Gift Huddle's own domains and links containing invisible formatting controls are blocked. The web gift table shows the validated URL for links it can open.
- A gift's link is now useful: in the gift editor (phone, tablet and web) an "Open link" button appears at the end of the link field when the text is a valid https link and opens it in the browser (never `http:`, `javascript:` or other schemes); in the web table the link icon is clickable and opens the link; on phones and tablets the gift row shows the link as its site (for example "amzn.eu") and opens it when tapped. If the browser cannot be opened, the link is copied instead.
- Pasting a link into the gift editor fills an empty Store field with the shop recognised from its address (Amazon, El Corte Inglés, Decathlon, IKEA, Zara, AliExpress, Fnac, MediaMarkt, PcComponentes, Carrefour, Toys R Us, Lego, Etsy, eBay, Apple, Nike, Adidas, H&M, Primark, Juguettos, Imaginarium, Casa del Libro, Shein, Temu), without going online; what you type in Store is never overwritten.
- Web: the link icon in the gift table is no longer cut off; it is now the standard Tabler "link" icon.
- Web, a person's page: the household budget card now spans the width of the gifts table below it (up to 1200 px on very wide windows, left-aligned with the table). Its bars are thicker (10 px) with small rounded ends, and when the card appears the bars fill from empty to their value once (about 0.6 s) while the amount rolls up; with Reduce motion they appear already full.
- Web, a person's page: the filter buttons line up with the avatar, the budget card and the gifts table (they started 4 px to the left).
- Web: hovering the status circle of a gift (in the gifts table, Today, occasions and Who gives what) shows its name ("Idea", "Decided", "Bought"...) in a tooltip; clicking the circle still changes the status.
- The household block on a person's page and the total in the tablet and desktop Summary column read "Spent €45" (no bar) instead of "€45 of €0" when nothing is planned.
- Budget, By person: a person with spent money but no planned price now reads "Spent €45" (no bar) instead of "€45 of €0", on the phone, the tablet and the web. Tablets: with the system font at 200 %, the navigation rail labels (People, Household...) no longer touch the rail edge.
- Web: Today, Who gives what and the sidebar no longer overflow with 200 % text at laptop widths (1280 to 1440 px); the "New person" label wraps and the giver names header grows to fit two lines.
- Occasions can have an optional budget, which the family sets in the occasion editor (for example Christmas 500 EUR, Kings 200 EUR); leave it empty for no budget.
- Budget: the three figures are now Budget, Spent and Left, with a line saying what you would have left (or go over by) if you buy what is still missing. Each occasion card shows what is spent of its budget and a button to set or change it; tapping an occasion or a person opens their gifts. Occasions without a budget say "No budget" and never show "Over by". Same on the phone, the tablet and the web.
- Budget: people never show "Over by" (only occasions have a budget), "Over by" now carries a small warning icon and is easier to read on the pastel cards in dark mode, archived occasions open their own gifts, and a family with occasions but no amounts can set its first budget (or create an occasion if it has none). The line under the title now says that spent is the real price, or the planned one if there is none.
- Budget stays accessible when every occasion is archived, and the first budget can be saved while another device's changes arrive. The subtitle explains which prices count as spent. Date pickers open on older saved dates, and the web keeps synced details when changing a date and follows the open occasion in the strip. Budget and gift price fields reject pasted text containing multiple amounts.
- Budget: the top figures (Budget, Spent, Left) and the "if you buy what is missing" line now count only occasions with a budget when at least one has one (otherwise Spent still shows all household spending), with a separate line for what was spent elsewhere ("The household spent another X outside budgeted occasions"), and when "By occasion" is shown it lists every active occasion, also the empty ones, so the first budget can be set there (with no budget or amount at all you still see the empty state, with "Set a budget", or "Create an occasion" if there is no active occasion). Same on the phone, the tablet and the web.
- Household budgets no longer count archived occasions: the Budget, Spent and Left figures, the "spent outside" line, By person and the household total ignore them (and their pending gifts), and an archived occasion still shows its full figures in its own row under "By occasion", tagged "Archived · not counted". Restoring the occasion brings its amounts back. Gifts synced for a deleted occasion appear under "No occasion".
- Occasion budgets and gift prices accept pasted amounts with spaces between groups of thousands, including non-breaking spaces.
- Tablets, People: the next-occasion row above the list of people (the one with the days in a square, e.g. "Leo's birthday") and the compact next-occasion card in the Summary column grow with large text instead of overflowing by a few pixels.
- The keyboard no longer pops up by itself after picking a date or an option in the gift, person and occasion editors.
- Gift prices are never cut or broken in two on phones and tablets, even with a long title and large text: the title wraps and the price keeps its full width (it only shrinks as a whole if it would take more than 60% of the row).
- Android: pressing a key (a hardware keyboard, or backspace and enter on some on-screen keyboards) no longer leaves a grey veil over the whole app.
- Occasions on phones and tablets are listed in one list by days left, in the same order as Today, instead of grouped into celebrations and birthdays; past one-off occasions go last, by name.
- Animations: when someone has everything bought, their initial square beats once (from 1 to 1.08) as the lavender tick appears, in People and on their page; with Reduce motion it just shows the tick.
